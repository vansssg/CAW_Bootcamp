# Module 01 quick pass (SkillSwap)

Not the final matrix. Warm-up only.

## 5 explicit requirements

1. Learners browse providers by category.
2. Learners view provider profiles that show ratings.
3. Learners book time slots and pay through the platform.
4. Search should feel instant.
5. Platform takes 15% commission on earnings.

## 2 ambiguities I am least sure about

1. What numeric SLA is “feel instant” (100ms vs 500ms) and at what concurrent search load?
2. Is 15% commission fixed for every category, or can it vary?

## 1 question for the PM right now

When a learner cancels, do we authorize-and-capture on book, or hold funds until the session starts — and which of those does “provider’s cancellation policy” mutate?
