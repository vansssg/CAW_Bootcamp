Why This Matters In Production (2 Minutes)

The moment you add retries, you risk double-counting.

​​‌​‌‌​‌‌​​​‌‌‌‌The moment you add a queue, you add operational failure modes (queue down, worker down).

​​​​‌‌​‌‌‌‌​‌‌‌‌This module makes those tradeoffs explicit and verifiable.
