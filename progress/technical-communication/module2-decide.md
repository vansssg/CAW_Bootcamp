# Module 2 DECIDE — document structure

Choice: **A top_down** (`decisions.module_02.document_structure = top_down`)

## Who I am writing for

Mixed authsvc readers: a VP with two minutes, an on-call engineer, and a new hire. Knight Capital is the constraint: if they stop after two sentences they must still have the action (which lever / which recommendation).

## Why A

A newspaper lede: conclusion first, evidence under it. VP gets the answer. New engineer can keep reading the body. Bottom-up is a detective story — if they stop at paragraph two they have clues and no answer, which is worse than useless during an incident.

## Tradeoff accepted

Some readers want WHY before WHAT. Mitigation (not a second stored choice): one-sentence recommendation, then a short “why it is true” paragraph, then details. Summary top-down, body can narrate. Stored style is still top_down because the first sentence must be the action.

## Rejected B for BUILD

Bottom-up educates well for a controversial RFC. This module’s bar is scannable prose after Knight Capital. A 45-minute fire does not wait for the detective ending.
