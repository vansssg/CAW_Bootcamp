​​​Quality Check: Remediation Items

‌​​The Blameless Culture Takeaway

Write one sentence about what blameless culture means to you now -- not the definition I gave you in Step 1, but your own understanding based on what you experienced writing this postmortem.

​​​The hardest moment in writing a blameless postmortem is when you know who wrote the bug. ‌​‌And you have to write about it without naming them, because the point is not who made the mistake. ​​​The point is why the system allowed the mistake to matter.
