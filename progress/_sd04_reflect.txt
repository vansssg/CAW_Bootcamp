Decision Callback

In the DECIDE step, students chose between API key authentication and JWT authentication.

‍​‍​​​​If they chose A (API key):
- The challenge they encountered was a consequence of API keys being long-lived static secrets that are easy to misconfigure or accidentally expose
- Connect it: "API keys are simple to implement but simple to misuse — a weak secret, a missing header check, or a rate-limit gap (like the one you just fixed) all stem from the fact that a static key carries no built-in expiry or scope enforcement."

​​​​​​​If they chose B (JWT):
- The challenge they encountered was a consequence of JWT verification depending on secret configuration and token validation logic that can be skipped or misconfigured
- Connect it: "JWTs add expiry and claims, but the bug you just debugged likely showed that those protections only work if the guard actually validates the signature and checks the right fields — a weak or missing check is just as dangerous as no auth at all."

‌​‌​‌​‌Ship Recap (1 Minute)

- What we shipped: a protected admin surface with a clear identity and a public redirect surface.
​‌​‌‌‌​- Remaining risk: auth without proper authorization rules and rate limits is still vulnerable to abuse, so we tighten those next.
